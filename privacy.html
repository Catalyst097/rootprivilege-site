Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Local-First Privacy

RootPrivilege Pro is built on a privacy-first, local-first architecture. All app installations, registry scans, and LAN deployments occur directly on your local network. We do not collect, store, or sell personal files, browsing logs, or sensitive machine telemetry.

2. Purchase Information

When you purchase a license, payment details (email, name, billing address) are securely handled by Lemon Squeezy. SystemicLogics only retains the customer email for license validation and critical update notices.

3. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact support@systemiclogics.com.
