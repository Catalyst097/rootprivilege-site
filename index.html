Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TwinWing and how it works.

Is TwinWing really 100% free?

Yes! You get full access to the 5,000+ app catalog, sequential install queue, updater, uninstaller, deep system cleaner, and local multi-PC fleet deployment for up to 8 computers completely free forever with no credit card or account needed.

How does the Zero-Cloud Fleet Deployer work?

It runs 100% locally over your Wi-Fi or Ethernet network using high-speed WebSockets (Port 8765) and UDP auto-discovery (Port 8766). No cloud servers, external databases, or third-party tracking are required.

How many computers are supported?

The Community Edition supports up to 8 connected workstations simultaneously across your local subnet. Larger multi-lab and enterprise tiers will be announced in future updates.

Does TwinWing collect my personal data?

No. TwinWing is strictly local-first. It does not send telemetry, device analytics, or usage metrics to external servers. All operations happen directly between your local machines.
