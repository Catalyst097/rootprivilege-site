Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Fleetwright and our software licensing.

Is Fleetwright really free for single PCs?

Yes! You get full access to the 5,000+ app catalog, sequential 1-click install queue, software updater, uninstaller, and deep cleaner completely free forever on your workstation.

How does the Zero-Cloud Fleet Deployer work?

It runs 100% locally over your Wi-Fi or Ethernet network using high-speed WebSockets (Port 8765) and UDP auto-discovery (Port 8766). No cloud servers, external databases, or third-party tracking are required.
